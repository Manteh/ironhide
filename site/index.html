macOS 15+ on Apple Silicon Orbie is a player and lookup tool. It doesn't host or index any content.

Getting in

Orbie is invite only. Anyone can download it, but it only opens with an invite from a member.

  1. Install. Paste this into Terminal. It puts Orbie in Applications and opens it.
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Manteh/orbie-app/HEAD/install.sh | bash
  2. Paste your invite. It looks like orbie-… and can sit inside a whole message.
  3. Say who you are. Continue with Discord, or pick a name and picture yourself.
  4. Connect a debrid account. TorBox is recommended. Use your own.
