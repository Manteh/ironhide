01  Tonight

It knows what you want tonight.

Open Orbie and it already knows what you were watching. Tell it the mood, tense or cozy or mind-bending, and the shelf rearranges itself around you.

Every pick carries a match score and says why it was chosen. It is built from your own ratings, and it never leaves your Mac.

Mood shelvesMatch scoresYour taste, kept local
02  The best version

Choose a version, not a file.

Every copy of a film is gathered in one place, duplicates merged and ranked for you: picture, sound, language, size and how fast it starts.

The best one is already chosen, with the reason beside it. Something lighter for Wi-Fi is one row down. Press Play and it begins.

4K · Dolby Vision · AtmosRanked for youStarts instantly
03  Search

Type. Press Enter. Watching.

Press Command and K anywhere in Orbie and start typing. Results appear as you type, Enter plays the first one and the right arrow opens its details.

No menus to dig through and no page to load first.

⌘K anywhereEnter plays→ opens details
04  Together

The same frame, everywhere.

Press play together and a countdown holds everyone until each person is ready. Then the film starts at the same moment, in sync to a fraction of a second, whether you share a couch or an ocean.

Live cursors and reactions drift across the picture. Everyone streams on their own Mac and only small messages travel between you, so no video is ever shared.

Ready countdownIn syncLive reactions
05  Craftsmanship

A room made with care.

Click the small orb in the top bar and the sanctuary opens: pixel plates, a short manifesto and a dedication to the people who make films. It is the one screen that exists only to say why Orbie exists.

Everything else in the app is built the same way. Slowly, and with the back of the cabinet finished.
