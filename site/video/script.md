# Orbie: motion graphics video

About 2 minutes at a relaxed pace (around 330 words). 16:9 first, then 9:16 and 1:1 cut from the same timeline.

## How to record
- Talk like you're showing it to a friend on your couch. It's fine to stumble a little, laugh, or change a word. Those takes usually sound best.
- One paragraph per take, with a second of silence before and after.
- Do each take twice. Don't read it, say it.
- Name the files `01-open.wav`, `02-taste.wav` and so on.
- If a sentence feels awkward in your mouth, say it your own way. Tell me and I'll update the script to match.

## The commentary

**01. Open** (about 12 s)
Okay, so this is Orbie. It's a little cinema that lives on your Mac. No ads, no endless rows of stuff you'll never watch, and nothing keeping track of you.

**02. It knows you** (about 20 s)
When I open it, it already knows where I left off. And if I'm in a mood, I just tell it. Tense, cozy, something that messes with my head. The whole shelf shuffles around that. Every pick even tells me why it picked it. And all of that, what I rate, what I watch, stays on my Mac. Nobody else gets it.

**03. The best version** (about 22 s)
Here's the part I like most. Say I pick a film. There are usually a lot of copies of it out there, and normally you'd have to dig through them yourself. Orbie just lines them up for me. Picture, sound, language, size. The best one is already picked, and it tells me why. I hit play, and it starts. That's it.

**04. Search** (about 8 s)
And if I already know what I want, I hit Command K, type a few letters, and press Enter. Done.

**05. Together** (about 30 s)
Now, when I want to watch with friends, nobody has to share their screen. Everyone's streaming on their own Mac, so it looks as good for them as it does for me. When we're all ready, there's a little countdown. Three, two, one. And it starts for everyone at the exact same moment, even if one of us is on the other side of the world. People can send reactions, and they float right across the picture. It honestly feels like being in the same room.

**06. Made with care** (about 17 s)
I care about the small stuff. This little orb up here opens a page about why any of this exists. It's not a feature list, it's just what we believe. That films are worth watching properly, and that the software should get out of the way.

**07. Close** (about 20 s)
Orbie is invite only, on purpose. Everyone who's in came from someone who knows them, and your invite has their name on it. So if you know someone who's already using it, ask them. If you don't, that's okay. It's still early. Orbie. A private cinema for the people you trust.
